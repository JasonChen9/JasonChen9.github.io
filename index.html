Guangyu Xiang

Ph.D. Student in Data Science and Analytics · HKUST(GZ)

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science and Analytics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Xiaowen Chu. My research focuses on machine learning systems, particularly efficient and elastic systems for large language model training and inference. My broader interests include GPU computing, distributed systems, and communication–computation co-optimization.

Before joining HKUST(GZ), I received my master’s degree in Software Engineering from Peking University and my bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.
